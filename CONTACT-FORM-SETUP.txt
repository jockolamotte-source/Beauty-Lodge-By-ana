Beauty Lodge Contact Form Setup

The form on contact.html submits to:
beautylodgebyanna@gmail.com

It uses FormSubmit so this static website can send completed forms by email
without a custom web server.

IMPORTANT FIRST-TIME ACTIVATION:
1. Publish the website.
2. Open contact.html through the live website.
3. Submit one test message.
4. FormSubmit will send a confirmation email to beautylodgebyanna@gmail.com.
5. Open that email and approve/activate the form.
6. Submit a second test message and confirm it arrives correctly.

After activation, new contact form submissions will be emailed to the
Beauty Lodge inbox.

The success redirect is:
https://www.beautylodgebyanna.com/contact-success.html
