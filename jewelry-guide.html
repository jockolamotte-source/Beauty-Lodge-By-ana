Frequently Asked

Permanent jewelry, explained.

If your question is specific to a piece, event, or circumstance, use the contact page and Anna can help.

What is permanent jewelry?+
It is a custom-fit chain that is welded closed instead of using a traditional clasp. Beauty Lodge offers fittings for the wrist, ankle, or neck.
Does the weld touch my skin?+
No. The weld is quick and performed away from the skin while the piece is being closed.
Can permanent jewelry be removed?+
Yes. The chain can be gently snipped if you need or want to remove it, and a saved piece may be able to be re-welded at a future visit.
How long does a fitting take?+
Plan for approximately 15 minutes per person. Group events are scheduled around the estimated number of guests.
How much does it cost?+
Pricing varies based on the jewelry selected. You will be able to see the available options when you come in for your fitting or visit a pop-up.
Can I book permanent jewelry for a party?+
Yes. Beauty Lodge offers mobile permanent jewelry for select private parties, bridal events, birthdays, business pop-ups, client events, markets, and other group gatherings.
What if a piece breaks?+
Keep the chain and contact Beauty Lodge. Anna can review the piece and let you know what options are available for your specific jewelry.
