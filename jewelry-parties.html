How It Works

We bring the jewelry bar. You bring the people.

From the first inquiry to the final weld, the experience is designed to be easy for the host and memorable for every guest.

  1. 01

    Start here

    Tell us about the event

    Share the date, location, event type, and an approximate guest count.

  2. 02

    We plan

    Build the setup

    Anna confirms timing, space needs, power access, and the best flow for your group.

  3. 03

    Guests choose

    Pick a piece

    Each guest browses the available chains and accents, then chooses what feels like them.

  4. 04

    The spark

    Fit, weld, wear

    Every piece is custom sized and welded closed, creating a keepsake they leave wearing.
