The Experience

Custom fit, Forever yours.

Permanent jewelry turns choosing a chain into an experience. Your piece is selected, sized to you, and welded closed without a traditional clasp.

01

Choose

Browse current chains, charms, and style inspiration when you arrive.

02

Fit

Your piece is measured and adjusted so it sits exactly where you want it.

03

Weld

The chain is closed with a quick spark, creating a seamless finish with no clasp.

04

Wear

Leave with something personal, whether it marks a moment or simply fits your everyday style.
